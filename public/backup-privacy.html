The Whitley Captures Backups

Privacy notice for the business’s private website backup connection.

Purpose and access

This connection is intended for Rachel Whitley and her authorised website administrator to save recovery copies of The Whitley Captures website content and photographs from Sanity to Rachel’s business Google Drive every two weeks.

The connection requests Google’s drive.file permission. This permits it to create and manage files it creates, and files or folders explicitly selected for use with it. It does not grant access to every file in the Google account. It does not request access to Gmail, contacts or calendars.

How information is used

The backup process uses file names, folder identifiers, file sizes and checksums to store and verify recovery copies. Backup files are private and are used for website recovery. Google Drive data is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

The planned cloud job will run through the business’s GitHub organisation and store backup files in Google Drive. Connection credentials will be kept in restricted secret storage. Backup contents and credentials must not be published in the public website repository or job logs.

Retention and control

Backup files remain in the business Drive until Rachel or her authorised administrator removes them. Disconnecting the app stops its future access but does not delete existing backups. Rachel can revoke access in her Google Account’s third-party connections settings, and delete backup files in Drive.

Contact

For questions, access removal or deletion requests, contact thewhitleycaptures@gmail.com.

This notice concerns the backup connection. The website’s privacy policy covers the public website.
